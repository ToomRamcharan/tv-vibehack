# TCL Smart TV Pro
# Platform: MediaTek MT9618 (tcl9618), Android 14 (API 34, Build UTT2.250416.001 AU02)
#
# IMPORTANT SAFETY NOTICE:
# DO NOT DISABLE 'com.google.android.apps.tv.launcherx'.
# On Android 14 Google TV, disabling the stock launcher triggers RescueParty during
# Direct Boot (Credential Encrypted storage lock), resulting in a recovery boot loop.
# tv-vibehack uses HomeKeyService (AccessibilityService) to intercept the Home button
# and display the custom launcher safely without disabling the stock launcher.
#

#   HDMI 2: com.tcl.tvinput/.TvPassThroughService/HW16
#   HDMI 3: com.tcl.tvinput/.TvPassThroughService/HW17
#   HDMI 4: com.tcl.tvinput/.TvPassThroughService/HW18
