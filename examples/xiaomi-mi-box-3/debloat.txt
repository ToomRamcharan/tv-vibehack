# Xiaomi Mi Box 3 (MIBOX3, codename once, Amlogic gxl), Android TV 9, firmware PI.3934
# Also a likely starting point for other Android 9 Xiaomi boxes with the stock Google TV launcher.
#
# Apply:   TV_HOST=<tv ip> tools/disable.sh examples/xiaomi-mi-box-3/debloat.txt
# Undo:    TV_HOST=<tv ip> tools/restore.sh examples/xiaomi-mi-box-3/debloat.txt
# Everything is DISABLED, not uninstalled. Comment out any line you want to keep.
#
# Stock launcher: com.google.android.tvlauncher. On Android 9 `cmd package set-home-activity` doesn't
# stick, so the custom launcher becomes Home by disabling the stock one (below). This is safe here:
# Settings' FallbackHome stays as a backup. Do NOT do this on Android 14 Google TV (launcherx).
# HomeKeyService (accessibility intercept) does nothing on this box; it isn't needed.
#
# No HDMI inputs, so the launcher hides its Inputs button and Netflix / Prime Video open normally.

# Google home screen and its recommendation rows
com.google.android.tvlauncher                  # stock launcher; re-enable to undo the custom Home
com.google.android.tvrecommendations

# Preloaded apps nobody asked for
com.google.android.youtube.tvmusic
com.google.android.videos                      # Google Play Movies & TV
com.google.android.play.games
com.android.camera2                            # no camera on a TV box
